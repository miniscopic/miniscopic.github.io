Premium and ad breaks

Premium turns off the short ad breaks between word cards. Every deck is free with or without it.

Restore Premium on a new phone: in Words.exe, open the Start menu, tap Go ad-free (or Premium), then Restore purchases.

Cancel or change your plan: on iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. You can also tap Manage subscription on the Premium page.

Moving to a new phone

Open the You panel and tap Get a transfer code, then enter the code on the new phone. Your home, Sparkles, things you own and your room on the street all move over.

Deleting your account

Open the You panel and tap Delete my account. Deleting the app alone doesn't delete your online account.

進階會員和廣告時間

進階會員會關閉單字卡之間的短暫廣告時間。不論是否訂閱，所有牌組都免費。

在新手機上恢復進階會員：在 Words.exe 打開「開始」選單，點「免廣告」（或「進階會員」），再點「恢復購買」。

取消或更改方案：iPhone 請打開「設定」，點你的名字，再點「訂閱項目」。Android 請打開 Google Play，點你的個人資料，再點「付款和訂閱」。你也可以在進階會員頁面點「管理訂閱」。

搬到新手機

打開「你」面板，點「取得轉移代碼」，再到新手機上輸入。你的家、星光、擁有的東西和街上的房間都會一起搬過去。

刪除帳號

打開「你」面板，點「刪除我的帳號」。只刪除 App 並不會刪除你的線上帳號。

隱私權
